## Implementation Planning Protocol

1. **Investigate Codebase & Dependencies**:
   - Inspect relevant existing modules, models, services, controllers, and tests using search tools.
   - Understand existing abstractions, data structures, and invariants.

2. **Produce Structured Plan**:
   Generate a detailed, actionable plan containing:
   - **Executive Summary**: Core goal and background context.
   - **User Review Required**: Breaking changes, critical architecture choices, or permission/data migrations.
   - **Technical Design & Architecture**: Detailed breakdown of components, data flow, and interfaces.
   - **Step-by-Step Task Breakdown**:
     - Atomic, sequential, verifiable tasks.
     - Specific files to create, modify, or delete.
   - **Verification & Test Plan**: Exact automated commands (`mvn test`, `npm test`, lint, etc.) and manual verification steps.
   - **Risk Assessment & Rollback Strategy**: Potential failure modes and mitigation.

3. **Constraints**:
   - Deliver clear, actionable Markdown.
   - Keep tasks fine-grained and logically ordered.
